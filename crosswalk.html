Method
Every one of the framework's 145 KSAs was drafted against v2.2.0 by model, citing one to four NICE statements per row and naming a primary work role. The draft was then triaged in two tiers: a mechanical tier that pre-accepted rows whose mapping was unambiguous, and a judgment tier that re-read the contestable ones and either affirmed them or set them aside. Across all 145 drafted rows the split was 42 Rob-reviewed, 70 auto-high and 33 auto-medium-affirmed. Rob read his 42 individually — accepting 41 and rejecting one as a duplicate — alongside a blind 10-row spot-check of the pre-accepted set, all ten of which he accepted. Rob's rulings are final and override the automated tiers; the automated decision stands wherever he did not rule. Two rows were then merged away (see the tombstones below), so the 143 rows in the table carry 41 Rob-reviewed, 70 auto-high and 32 auto-medium-affirmed decisions. The deciding tier is recorded per row in the page source and in the published decision record; it is no longer shown as its own column.

Footnotes — where NICE has no equivalent

These are recorded as footnotes rather than forced into a nearest-fit remap. The gap is in the target framework.

  1. K-MOB01, S-MOB01, A-FOR01 — NICE Framework Components v2.2.0 contains no statement mentioning mobile devices, MDM, BYOD or mobile platforms. Verified by full-corpus search of all 2,211 statements. These rows are reachable only through platform-agnostic forensics, and K-MOB01 has no NICE equivalent at all.
  2. K-GEO01 — v2.2.0 contains no geolocation, badge-correlation or triangulation statement. The mapping rests on the nearest general analytic statements, which is why its confidence is low.
  3. K-H01, S-H02, A-H01, A-H02 — NICE has no threat-hunting concept. These four rows resolve to anomaly identification plus signature development. That is a gap in NICE, not a weak mapping.
  4. A-AGENT01, K-AI01 — The agentic-AI and AI statements sit in Competency Area NF-COM-002 (AI Security), which is not a work role. v2.2.0 has no work role that owns them, so these rows cite statements without an owning role.
