Community Contributors

This framework is built by insider threat practitioners, for insider threat practitioners. The people listed here suggested resources, reported corrections, or otherwise helped make it more useful. These are informational contributions — no money changes hands.

Contributors
🔒
FS-ISAC Insider Threat Working Group
Original framework development, peer review, KSA validation, and ongoing feedback across multiple iterations.
🏛️
CMU SEI — OSIT Working Group
The Open Source Insider Threat Information Sharing Working Group (250+ members, 130+ organizations) is the SEI-moderated community of practice for industry-based insider threat program practitioners. The framework's Custom KSAs draw heavily on research published by the SEI's CERT Division and National Insider Threat Center. Presenting to OSIT was a milestone for the project — their feedback continues to shape its direction.
🧠
Dr. Frank Greitzer
Creator of SOFIT 2.0 (Sociotechnical and Organizational Factors for Insider Threat). Suggested the annotated bibliography page and mitigation strategy mapping tied to threat types. Foundational researcher in psychosocial precursors of insider threat risk.
🧭
Jack O'Halloran & Matthew Davies
Independently suggested mapping training and certifications to KSAs — the idea that became the competency clusters system. Two practitioners asked the same question: "I can see what I need to know, but how do I get there?" This feature exists because of them.
🧠
Bidemi Ologunde, PhD, CICA
Suggested cloud-collaboration telemetry as a first-class insider signal source (refined A-SYNTH01), contributed the CISA Microsoft Expanded Cloud Logs Playbook, and identified AI agents as insider-like access holders — an emerging threat vector documented in the AI/ML cluster and governance model. Returned with the investigative half of that question — how an analyst attributes and reconstructs what an agent actually did — which became A-AGENT01, along with the sources behind it.
📊
Mark Handy
Co-author of Stewart & Handy (2024) insider threat maturity model. Suggested the UMD Graduate Certificate in Insider Risk Management, INSA resource library expansion, IRPA/CoE cross-references, AI/ML training platform resources, and alphabetical ordering improvements. Originated the how2TTX idea behind the TTX Builder, and suggested the Insider Risk Events community calendar, now run by the US InRM CoE.
⭐
Rowland Bartlett
Suggested the "Top 5" priority KSA filter — the idea that each level should highlight the five most important competencies to focus on first. Now a core navigation feature on the framework page.
🌍
Nicholas Tuppen
SVP, Global Information Security, Bank of America. Contributed the IRRG publications list — EMEA-focused insider risk resources from NPSA, NCSC, SIFMA, and UK cross-sector working groups. Coordinates the UK Cross Market Operational Resilience Group (CMORG) Insider Risk Research Group.
📰
Tim Schnurr
Suggested DataTheftNews — trade secret protection and insider threat prevention news resource. Also contributed the Insider Threat Framework & Maturity Assessment open-source GitHub project (LeastTrust).
📋
Justin Estadt
Suggested INSA insider threat white paper series.
🤖
Oswin Kwok
Suggested ISACA AAISM (Advanced in AI Security Management) as a cert anchor for the AI/ML cluster — AI governance and program management complement to practitioner-level tools. Also compiled the multi-jurisdiction Regulatory Mandates for Insider Threat Programs reference list (US, DoD, NRC, NIST, Canada, Australia, Singapore, UK, and NERC authorities).
🎖
Anonymous
Suggested DoD 8140.03 — Cyberspace Workforce Qualification & Management Program.
📝
Kasey Olbrych
Proofpoint. Suggested the Program Auditor concentration and the Audit & Assessment cluster — bringing program audit-readiness into the framework as a distinct concentration alongside the operational tracks.
⚖️
Shawn M. Thompson
Founder of ITMG (Insider Threat Management Group) — a licensed attorney and former federal prosecutor. Provided the canonical links for ITMG's Insider Risk Body of Knowledge™ and Insider Risk & Insider Threat Capability Framework™ (IRCF™ v1.0), corrected the IRCF name (Capability, not Control), and pointed practitioners to free e-book access for his 90-day plan (see annotated bibliography).
🎓
Jessica Wren
Suggested the CPE Resources page.
🧠
Dr. Deanna D. Caputo
Chief Scientist for Insider Threat Capabilities, The MITRE Corporation. Identified that the framework presented two distinct MITRE efforts as a single resource — MITRE's insider threat research and behavioral risk work, and MITRE CTID's ATT&CK-based TTP work. Now split into separate entries on Start Here.
🍁
Victor Munro
Executive Director of the Canadian Insider Risk Management Centre of Excellence (CInRM CoE). Suggested adding the CoE's practical and professional certificates in insider risk management — built with Carleton University's Norman Paterson School of International Affairs and carrying ASIS-recognized CPE credits — to the CPE Resources page.
🔎
Timothy Carreira
Suggested adding Insider Intel — insider-threat intelligence distilled from real court cases — to the Research & vendor intelligence resources on the Program Hub.
🗺️
Alessandro Aledda
Author of INTRA (Insider Threat and Risk Architecture) — a European jurisdiction layer for insider risk, 110 measures across nine pillars mapped to 34 jurisdictions with a read source on every row, published free under CC BY 4.0. Added to the standards and professional bodies resources on the Program Hub. Reviewed this site and called it the best map of the field he has found.
