Standing courses, certificates and degree programs for people who work in insider risk, or want to — from free self-paced modules to full graduate degrees. Entries come from US universities, university-affiliated programs, the US federal government, professional bodies and international providers. Each one links to the provider and carries a marker showing whether it has been confirmed on the institution's own website.

How to read this directory

  • Adjacent fields are included, and labeled. Insider risk borrows from neighboring disciplines, so the directory also lists courses in behavioral threat assessment, fraud and financial crime, and personnel security and counterintelligence. Every entry shows its field. An entry whose own page does not name insider threat carries an adjacent field tag: it is listed because it belongs to a recognized adjacent discipline, such as threat assessment and management or security management, or appears on the coursework reference this directory grew from.
  • Runs as scheduled cohorts means the program is standing but delivered in dated sittings — check the provider for the next one.
  • Component courses sit inside the program they belong to. Open a program's component courses to see them.
  • Credit means academic credit toward a degree. Cost not stated means the provider does not publish a price where we looked — not that the course is free.

Notes and corrections

  • NITTF Insider Threat Hub Operations Course. The course was sunset by NITTF Advisory 2021-002, but course flyers for CY2024, CY2025 and CY2026 are published, so the course is live. Separately, dni.gov now redirects NITTF pages to archive.dni.gov, which carries an “archived” banner: the course is live; its custodian's web presence is archived.

Known gaps

  • No standing EU-institution program. The closest match is CEPOL, the EU agency for law enforcement training. In 2024 it ran an activity titled “Insider Threat” (45/2024/ONS), held in the Czech Republic for law enforcement officials and focused on radicalisation within law enforcement. That was a single dated activity, not a standing program, so it is not listed. The European presence in the international group is the IAEA, which is multilateral, not an EU body.
  • Vendor academies reviewed, none listed. Checked in September 2026 against the inclusion test — substantive rather than a product pitch. Everfox publishes webinars and blog content but no training program. DTEX's i³ training is enablement for its own platform. ITMG's Academy is listed above as forthcoming.
  • Candidate: ITMG Academy courses. ITMG’s Program Development and Operations Training was removed in September 2026: its page no longer exists, and ITMG’s site now points to the ITMG Academy, announced as “Coming Soon • Fall 2026”. Individual Academy courses will be considered for listing once they are published.
  • AIR CoE Insider Risk for Executives Workshop is linked from the Australian Cyber Collaboration Centre's menu, but that link returned “page not found” in September 2026, so the workshop is not listed on its own.
  • See also: Training, Education and Awareness, a curated listing maintained by the Insider Risk Practitioner Alliance (IRPA).
